As my schedule this fall is quite full, I cannot promise to take on any new commissions until the new year, but if you have a project in mind, please don't hesitate to reach out!

The process works like this: You have a need or an idea and reach out, hurray! I ponder your idea, and if I have the bandwidth, we will meet for a 30–45 minute design consultation. If we both agree, I will then create two–four design sketches at a variety of price points for you to choose between. Once you've chosen a direction and we've agreed on a timeline, a 50% deposit of the estimate is due before I will begin fabrication.

For stained glass panels and most sculptural projects, pricing is standardized:

$10 per piece within the work, $50 per square foot of the finished piece for standard glass, and the direct cost of the frame. For extremely complicate pieces, extended design work, or specialty glass, I can offer a range of estimates only as the cost of glass fluctuates with the market price of gold, silver, and metal commodities.

For example: a 12" x 12" piece with 100 individual pieces within the design will cost $10 x 100 pieces ($1000) + $50 x 1 sq ft, + >$15 for ~4' of framing.

For innovative construction or custom-built light boxes, the price will likely be significantly higher than for panels, as it will require significantly more labor and design time. 

I am excited to hear about your projects, and if I cannot help you, I may be able to direct you to another artist who can!

I look forward to hearing from you!
